01

About

I work at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and applied software engineering, with a focus on Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models. My work spans machine learning and data analysis, designing LLM-based and multi-agent systems, and building the interfaces and data pipelines that turn those models into products people actually use.

A large part of my work is AI-based educational systems: designing LLM-powered agents that guide learners through reasoning, reflection, and scientific inquiry, and evaluating how well they teach. That research sits alongside years of hands-on teaching and mentoring — tutoring machine learning, programming, and OOP at the University of Haifa, and mentoring high-school students through their own AI projects.

I care about systems that are careful with their prompts, honest about their evaluation, and genuinely useful to the person on the other side of the screen.
